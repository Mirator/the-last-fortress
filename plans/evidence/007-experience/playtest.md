# 007 Stage E: player validation

Status: **PENDING. No participants yet.** Nobody outside the project has played the plan 007 build. An agent running a simulation script cannot stand in for a participant, so nothing below has been measured. Recruiting is up to the owner; this task contacted no one.

## What to run

- Build: copy the whole `game/The Last Fortress/Builds/Mvp/` folder, not just the `.exe`. The version and build id show at the bottom of the title menu; record them for each session.
- Each participant gets a fresh save folder, so the guide shows: `TheLastFortress.exe -saves <empty folder>`. Never use your own saves.
- Play with sound on, at normal speed. Before the session, do one listening pass yourself (checklist below).

## Session protocol (five people who have never played the game)

Don't coach, hint or explain. If someone asks, answer "What do you think?" and note the question.

| # | Task | Record |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Start the game and survive the first night | Wall-clock time to first valid Tower; to Start Night; whether they found Help / Skip; every hesitation over 10 s and where it happened |
| 2 | "How do enemies decide where to go? Where does your gold come from?" | Their own words. Mark correct / partial / wrong |
| 3 | Day 2: "Anything new tonight?" | Did they notice the East entry and the Runner announcement unprompted? |
| 4 | After a loss (or Night 4): "What went wrong?" | Did they name a concrete placement consequence? |
| 5 | Choose a perk: "What will it change?" | Correct / wrong / guessing |
| 6 | Outcome screen | Do they start another run without being asked? Why or why not? |

Also note the length of each Night and Day, stretches with no decision or no attention (looking away, idle mouse), and the total run length.

## Acceptance (plan 3.1, 3.5)

- At least 4 of 5 place a valid defense, start Night and explain the route within two minutes. Winning doesn't count.
- 5 of 5 find Help and can dismiss the guide.
- At least 4 of 5 describe a concrete consequence of a placement and say what they would change.
- At least 3 of 5 choose to replay (an indication, not retention evidence).

If 3.1 fails, simplify the guide copy or the HUD hierarchy first, then repeat the failed task with new participants. Change rules only for problems that persist with informed players. Write each rule change up first and compare the same seeds before and after (plan 3.5).

## Listening checklist (owner, before the sessions)

- Night 1, dense Night 4 and the Night 6 finale at normal speed. You should be able to pick out Keep damage. Listen for clipping, a flood of identical samples, and music drowning out threat sounds.
- Mute (Master 0): real silence. Restart the game and check the saved volumes come back. A settings file from an older build should load with default volumes.
- Pause with an arrow in flight: its impact must not sound until you resume. Open and close the menu repeatedly: the music must not stack.
